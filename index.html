    What is Alara Village?

    Alara Village is a garden community of two-storey homes in Cavite, Philippines, made for families to grow. It offers four house models, Veta, Zila, Raya and Yana, from 85 to 130 sqm. Every home has a lanai and two parking slots.

    What house models and lot sizes are available at Alara Village?

    Veta is an 85 sqm single detached home for first-time buyers and young couples. Zila is a 96 sqm partially attached home for growing families. Raya is a 110 sqm single attached home for multi-generational households. Yana is a 130 sqm single detached home, the largest in the village. The minimum lot cut is 121 sqm for Veta, Zila and Raya, and 171 sqm for Yana.

    What amenities and nearby places does Alara Village have?

    The village amenities are the Entrance Gate, Midpark 1, Forest Park, Midpark 2, the Play Area Tri-Court and the Clubhouse Pool. CALAX Open Canal exit is about 2 to 3 minutes away, SM City General Trias is about 5 to 8 minutes away, and South Imus Specialist Hospital is about 12 to 15 minutes away.
